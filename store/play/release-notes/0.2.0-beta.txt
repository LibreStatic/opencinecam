<en-US>
First closed beta of OpenCineCam.
- Manual ISO, shutter, white balance and focus, with zebra, peaking, false colour and scopes
- LOG and HLG video modes where the device supports them, with LUT monitoring
- WAV/FLAC audio, timecode and production slate
- Adaptive layouts for phones, tablets and foldables
Report problems at github.com/LibreStatic/opencinecam/issues
