OpenCineCam 1.0:
- Lens calibration: match the colour and exposure of every lens in preview and video
- Lens lock: keep a take on one lens while you zoom
- Smooth lens switch (optional) softens the framing jump between lenses
- FPS LOCK keeps the exposure steady when the lens switches
- Pinch zoom works even with a finger over a control
