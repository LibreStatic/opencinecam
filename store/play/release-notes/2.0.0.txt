- RAW video: qualify the phone or a USB drive, record and export CinemaDNG
- Record to a USB drive; takes cut by a pulled drive are repaired
- Raw mic input, input equaliser and headphone monitoring
- Volume-key gestures
- Slow motion: take length limit; scopes stay on
- Network drive: open your takes from a computer

- Front camera no longer upside down
