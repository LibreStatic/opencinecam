<en-US>
- Upload new takes automatically to your WebDAV server (off by default, Wi-Fi only)
- A take that fails is kept as an incomplete take instead of being deleted
- Slow motion: pick the resolution, and any rate above the project rate counts
- High-speed takes no longer lose frames when you stop
- Each video take saves a .clip.json with its settings
Report problems at github.com/LibreStatic/opencinecam/issues
